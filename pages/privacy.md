# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 16 June 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how the "PlayfulCEO" application ("we", "us") handles information when it interacts with the Instagram account **@playful_ceo** and people who engage with its posts. The app is operated by Aditya Jhunjhunwala. Contact: **adityaj@adipa.com**.

## What the app does

When someone comments a specific keyword (e.g. "PORTFOLIO") on a @playful_ceo post that invites them to, the app responds to that comment — by posting a public reply and/or sending that commenter a single private reply (Instagram Direct message) containing the free resource they requested. The app only acts in response to a person's own comment.

## Information we access

Through the Instagram Graph API, and only as needed to perform the above, the app accesses:

- **Public comment data** — the comment text, the commenter's Instagram username, the comment ID, and timestamp.
- **Comment-reply state** — whether the account has already replied to a comment (to avoid replying twice).
- **Message-send capability** — used solely to send a private reply to a person who commented.

We do **not** access your private messages, contacts, photos, or any data unrelated to responding to a comment.

## How we use it

- To detect the keyword in a comment and send the requested resource (a public reply and/or one DM).
- To avoid sending duplicate replies to the same comment.

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. We do not use it for advertising profiles, and we do not send unsolicited or recurring marketing messages.

## Data retention

We retain only the minimum needed to operate: the IDs of comments we have already replied to (so we don't reply twice) and basic run logs. Comment text is processed transiently to detect the keyword and is not retained beyond operational logs. We do not store the contents of Direct messages beyond delivery.

## Your choices and deletion

You can request deletion of any data associated with you at any time — see our [Data Deletion instructions](/data-deletion/). You can also delete your comment on Instagram, which removes it from our processing.

## Third parties

The app relies on Meta Platforms' Instagram Graph API and is subject to Meta's Platform Terms and Developer Policies. We send the resource link to the commenter; the linked page ([/portfolio/](/portfolio/)) has its own terms.

## Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date reflects the latest version.

## Contact

Questions or requests: **adityaj@adipa.com**.
