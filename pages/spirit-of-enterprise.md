# Spirit of Enterprise

A podcast of long conversations with people who build things. It rests on one idea I keep coming back to: good people must succeed. When good people make money, they do good things with it. Business, done well, is a force for good.

Five conversations are recorded. They come out one at a time.

---

## Episode 1. Namita Thapar

<p style="color:var(--text-muted); margin-top:-0.5rem;">Executive Director, Emcure Pharmaceuticals. Judge on Shark Tank India.</p>

I asked her what being a good person means in business. Her answer is the line I have not stopped thinking about.

<blockquote style="font-family:var(--font-serif); font-style:italic; font-size:1.35rem; color:var(--navy); border-left:3px solid var(--gold); padding:0.25rem 0 0.25rem 1.25rem; margin:1.5rem 0;">"My report card is whether I sleep with peace in my heart."</blockquote>
