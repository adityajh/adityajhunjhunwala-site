# Privacy Policy: Composio Sheets

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how the "Composio Sheets" application ("the app", "we") handles Google user data. The app is operated by Aditya Jhunjhunwala for his own use. Contact: **adityajh@gmail.com**.

## What the app does

Composio Sheets is a private Google OAuth app. It lets Aditya Jhunjhunwala's own AI assistant and automation tools, connected through the Composio integration platform, read and update spreadsheets in his own Google account. It is not offered to the public, and the only Google account it is used with is the operator's own.

## Google user data we access

With the account owner's consent, the app requests these Google permissions:

- **Google Sheets** (`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets`): to read, create and edit spreadsheets the account owner asks the assistant to work on.
- **Google Drive, read only** (`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly`): to find and open existing spreadsheets by name or folder. The app does not modify, delete or share Drive files through this permission.

## How we use it

Google user data is used only to carry out tasks the account owner requests, such as reading figures from a sheet, adding rows or updating a tracker. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not used to build user profiles or to train AI models.

## Sharing

Data passes only between Google and the tools that perform the requested task: the Composio platform, which stores the OAuth tokens and makes the API calls, and the AI assistant the account owner is using. We do not share Google user data with any other third party.

## Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Retention and deletion

We do not keep copies of spreadsheet contents beyond what is needed to complete a task. OAuth tokens are held by Composio until access is revoked. You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), or ask us to delete any data by writing to the address below.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.

## Contact

Questions or requests: **adityajh@gmail.com**.
