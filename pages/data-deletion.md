# Data Deletion Instructions

_Last updated: 16 June 2026_

The "PlayfulCEO" application (operated by Aditya Jhunjhunwala) interacts with the Instagram account **@playful_ceo** to respond to comments with a public reply and/or a single private reply (DM). It stores only minimal operational data — chiefly the IDs of comments it has already replied to, so it does not reply twice.

## How to request deletion of your data

You have the right to have any data associated with you deleted. To request it:

1. **Email us at adityaj@adipa.com** with the subject line **"Data Deletion Request"**.
2. Include your **Instagram username** so we can locate and remove any stored reference (e.g. the comment IDs associated with your interactions).

We will delete the associated data and confirm by reply within **30 days**.

## Self-service

You can also simply **delete your comment** on the Instagram post — this removes it from any further processing by the app.

## What we delete

- Stored comment IDs / references associated with your account.
- Any operational log entries that reference your username.

We do not retain Direct message contents beyond delivery, and we never sell or share your data.
