## Workshops and keynotes

I have spoken at and run workshops for schools and colleges across India, the Bohra community leadership forum, MCCIA Pune (Family Business Leadership Summit), YI (Young Indians), IIM-A new entrant programs, NGOs, and corporate leadership teams.

Topics include entrepreneurial mindset, building a body of work, education reform, self-awareness for founders, and leadership culture.
