# About

Work should be a source of happiness and freedom.

Life should be filled with food, play and love.

Every moment should be gratitude, ease and flow.

---

I help founders and future founders do the one thing nobody teaches: **see themselves clearly.**

Most people who come to me are stuck. Not because they lack strategy or skills. But because they are performing someone else's version of success. When they stop and look inward, the answers they were searching for were already there. I call this the real work. It is the method behind everything I do.

I am the founder of **[Let's Enterprise](https://www.letsenterprise.in)**, India's first Working BBA. For the last 20 years I have worked with founders ranging from 19-year-olds to 100-crore CEOs, with educational institutions, and with thousands of young people.

I am also the author of **Restless to Remarkable**, a TEDx speaker (twice), and the writer of a weekly letter called Eat. Play. Love. which has been going out every Sunday since 2020.
