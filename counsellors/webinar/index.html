What we will do together

Three tools, run live on real cases.

This is a working session, not a lecture. Each tool takes about 20 minutes: what it is, a real (anonymised) student case, and how you would run it on Monday.

1Open

The 1,095-day swap

Three years, in hours

When a parent asks "which college?", change the question to how the next three years will be spent.

How will you spend the next 17,520 waking hours?
2Map

Thaali map: four rooms

Who lets you in, and is there a seat?

Two questions sort any career into Ladder, Double Gate, Craft or Risk, and show what a student has to get good at in each.

Who lets you in, and is there a seat when you get good?
3Act

The 30-day proof sprint

Something finished in 30 days

Turn "I want to make vlogs" into a number, a format and a date. A task you can set a student the same week.

What can you show me, finished, in 30 days?
