The 90 minutes

10:00

Why the question has changed

Ten minutes on what families are actually worried about now, and what that asks of us.

10:10Tool 1 of 3

The 1,095-day swap

“How will you spend the next 17,520 waking hours?”

When a parent asks which college, swap the question for how the next three years will be spent. Class takes about a tenth of them. The rest decides who the student becomes.

On Monday: sit with a family and fill in the hours budget together. It moves the conversation from brands to how the time is used.

10:30Tool 2 of 3

The thaali map

“Who lets you in, and is there a seat when you get good?”

Two questions sort any career into one of four rooms. Each room asks a student to get good at something different, which is exactly what a shortlist of colleges never tells them.

On Monday: place a student's top three career ideas on the map. If all three sit in the Risk room, that is the conversation to have first.

10:50Tool 3 of 3

The 30-day proof sprint

“What can you show me, finished, in 30 days?”

“I want to make vlogs” becomes a number, a format and a date. Finished work tells a student, and later an admissions panel or an employer, more than any interest test.

On Monday: set one student a 30-day sprint and book the review now. You will learn more from day 30 than from the whole first meeting.
