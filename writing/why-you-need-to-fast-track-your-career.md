1. Early specialisation: With the rise of technology and automation, the demand for specialized skills has increased. To keep up with this demand, young professionals are encouraged to start specialising in their field as early as possible, which requires them explore themselves and the world sooner, faster and arrive at a decision about what to specliase in. Hence they need to fast track their career.
2. Shorter attention spans: Today's fast-paced lifestyle, combined with an abundance of distractions and information overload, has resulted in shorter attention spans. As a result, students and professionals need to be able learn new things on go, adapt to changing environments and make decisions at a faster pace, which requires a fast-tracked career.
3. Increased competition: The job market is more competitive than ever, with a larger pool of talented candidates vying for the same positions. To stand out in this crowded field, young professionals need to get experience and clarity quicker and be ready to take steps to fast track their career.
4. Higher expectations: Employers expect new hires to hit the ground running and make an immediate impact. This requires young professionals to gain experience and skills at a faster pace than previous generations, which necessitates a fast-tracked career.
5. Changing job market: The job market is constantly evolving, and professionals need to be able to adapt to new trends and technologies. This requires a willingness to learn and grow, as well as a proactive approach to career development, which can be achieved through a fast-tracked career.
