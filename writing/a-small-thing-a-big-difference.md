What’s the difference between coffee and black coffee?
What’s the difference between bread and toast?
What’s the difference between fresh lime water and fresh lime soda?
What’s the difference between school and college?

Milk.
Heat.
Fizz!
Uniforms!

There, thats the difference. It’s the presence or absence of one thing that makes all the difference.

So, what’s the difference between desperation and longing?
What’s the difference between recklessness and speed?
What’s the difference between turning a blind eye and forgiving ?

What’s the difference between lust and love?
What’s the difference between giving up and letting go?

What’s the difference between laziness and rest?
What’s the difference between greed and goals?
What’s the difference between guilt and repentance?
What’s the difference between dependance and interdependence?

Joy.
Control.
Courage.
Giving.
Faith.
Limits.
Inspiration.
Humility.
Completeness.

Its the presence of these that creates longing, speed, forgiveness, love, letting go, restfulness, goal focus, repentance, and interdependence.
