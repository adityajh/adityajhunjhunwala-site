Emergent Ventures

No degree gate

They fund ideas, not degrees, even at 16. A grant and fellowship for people building something new. Open to anyone 13 and up, anywhere, with a dedicated India track. Lightweight application, reviewed on a rolling basis.

mercatus.org/emergent-ventures

Deadline: rolling, apply anytime.

2

Z Fellows

No degree gate

A 10,000 dollar cheque and a week inside Silicon Valley. No degree, no company, no age limit. For early builders working on a project or startup, solo or as a team. Rolling cohorts.

zfellows.com

Deadline: rolling cohorts, apply anytime.

3

PM Internship Scheme

Get paid to work inside a top-500 Indian company. Around 9,000 a month plus a joining grant, for 6 to 9 months. Apply if you are 21 to 24 and not in a full-time job or course. Runs in phases through 2026.

pminternship.mca.gov.in

Deadline: check the portal for the current phase window.

4

Live hackathons (Devfolio + Unstop)

Build a real thing in a weekend. Win, or just keep the proof. Hundreds of live hackathons you can join right now, free. Pick one, form a team, ship by Sunday.

devfolio.co/discover and unstop.com/hackathons

Deadline: always live, each hackathon has its own date.

5

AICTE National Internship Portal

A lakh-plus real internships, one free login. Government portal, 200-plus fields, listings refresh constantly. Each role has its own date, so check and apply.

internship.aicte-india.org

Deadline: rolling, per listing.
