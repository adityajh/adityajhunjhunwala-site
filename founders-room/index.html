I'm a founder, and I spend my days coaching them. Most of my energy goes into Let's Enterprise, where we're building India's first working BBA: a degree where the work itself is the curriculum, and students spend 15 months apprenticing inside real businesses instead of sitting in lecture halls. One thing drives me, closing the gap between knowing and doing, so young people walk into work with proof rather than just a certificate.

I won't pretend I have AI figured out. I don't. That's the honest reason I asked Moumita to lead this, and pulled together a small room of founders I genuinely respect. I learn the way I suspect you do, by doing the real thing in good company and asking the questions out loud that we'd usually only ask in private. My job on Thursday is to hold that room, and to connect you to each other after.
