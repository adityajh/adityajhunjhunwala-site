Positioning

The single idea every piece of content must leave behind.

"The real work is you."
Intimate variant: "See yourself. Everything else follows."
Every piece of content must leave the audience with one implicit message: "The real work isn't out there. It's in here. Start with yourself."
Archetype

The Playful Provocateur. Warm but uncompromising. Direct. Conversational. Challenges sacred cows from care, not rage.

The method

Works inside-out. Help someone see themselves clearly (their real ambition, their real friction) and strategy, team, and culture become obvious.

Primary audience

Founders and future founders (18-60+). The person performing someone else's version of success and stuck because of it.

Connection to LE

LE's line: "Work is the curriculum." Adi's: "The real work is you." At LE, work is how you learn. With Adi, YOU are the work.

Authority plus play

The brand is a mix of authority and playfulness. Authority is the navy, the gold and the serif: a CEO who has done the work. Play is the handwriting, the teal and the wit: someone who will not take the work, or himself, too seriously. Every layout carries both. See Authority and Play.

Hard Rules

Non-negotiable. Applies to every agent, every draft, every platform.

  • TypographyNo em dashes or en dashes ever. In any content for posting. Replace with commas, periods, colons, or restructure. All platforms, all formats, including internal docs and code (grep built files for both characters before every deploy).
  • Let's EnterpriseThe 95/5 rule. 95% thought leadership. 5% LE mentions, only obliquely (a student, an anecdote). No admissions CTAs. Never "apply now."
  • DraftingAsk 2-3 sharp questions before writing anything. One must request a specific story or incident. No anchor = no draft.
  • Feed disciplineNo promotional posts on personal feed. LE event invites, admissions pushes: never on personal LinkedIn or Instagram.
  • CaseSentence case everywhere. All caps only for short labels and punch words.
  • PlayOne play moment per layout. One signature bar, at most one margin note. See Authority and Play.
  • SeriesNo series fatigue. Never run 3+ posts on the same external person. Decay confirmed by analytics by post 3.

Authority and Play

Authority is the default layer. Play is added on top with three devices, and only these.

Plans don't compound.
1 · The signature bar
The monogram's short rounded underline, under ONE key word in a headline. Heritage Gold on cream (a graphic, not text), Bright Gold on navy.
Find your passion.actually, no.
2 · The margin note
A handwritten Caveat aside that talks back to the serious headline: a correction, a crossed-out word, an "actually, no." Teal on cream, Teal Light on navy, tilted 2 to 4 degrees, never below 24px.
The perfect first draft.
3 · Teal
The play colour. It marks the playful layer in small strokes: margin notes, strike-throughs, small highlights. Never a large fill, always quieter than gold.
  • The limitOne play moment per layout: one bar, and at most one margin note. Play is always optional: where the setting needs 90% authority, drop the note and the teal and let the type carry it.
  • The testIf a layout feels like a bank, add the note. If it feels like a meme, take it out.

Color Do and Don't

Do

  • Start from cream, set type in navy, accent with gold.
  • Use Heritage Gold for print and letters, Bright Gold for social and screens.
  • Let gold be the loudest color. Use it sparingly so it stays special.
  • Put navy text on gold and teal-light fills, cream text on navy.
  • Keep teal for the play layer: small strokes, cooler and quieter than gold.

Don't

  • Introduce coral, red, orange, or any hue not on this page. (Confirmed ruled OUT.)
  • Set gold text on cream: 2.05:1, it fails accessibility.
  • Use white text on gold: 2.21:1, it also fails.
  • Let teal compete with gold or carry the headline.
  • Use Bright Gold on large fields. Small accent areas only.
  • Set teal text on cream below 24px (3.2:1), or use teal as a large fill or tag fill.

Keep no more than two families on any single layout.

LabelInter 600 · 12px caps · 0.08em
actually, no.Caveat · 24px and up · play colour

Type do

  • Two families per layout maximum. Serif for feeling, sans for clarity.
  • Sentence case everywhere, in line with the voice.
  • Body at 16px web with 1.6-1.7 line-height. Never below 14px.
  • Caveat is the margin note: one per layout, 24px and up. Lora italic for rare accents.

Type don't

  • Mix System A and System B fonts in the same asset.
  • Set long passages in ALL CAPS. Caps for short punch labels only.
  • Stack three or more typefaces on one piece.
  • Use DM Serif or Lato Black for tiny body text.

Logo and Mark

The "A" monogram: a serif capital A in Bright Gold with a short rounded underline bar, on a Navy Dark field. The bar is the same signature bar the headlines use.

Mark do

  • Use it as the favicon, avatar, app icon and small sign-off mark.
  • Always gold on its navy tile. Master vector: /favicon.svg.
  • Keep at least 16px of clear space around the tile.
  • Beside the mark, set the name in DM Serif Display, the handle in Inter.

Mark don't

  • Recolour it, or set it on cream without its navy tile.
  • Use it below 32px without checking the underline bar survives.
  • Add an icon set. Prefer words.
