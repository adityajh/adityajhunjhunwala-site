Positioning

The single idea every piece of content must leave behind.

"The real work is you."
Intimate variant: "See yourself. Everything else follows."
Every piece of content must leave the audience with one implicit message: "The real work isn't out there. It's in here. Start with yourself."
Archetype

The Playful Provocateur. Warm but uncompromising. Direct. Conversational. Challenges sacred cows from care, not rage.

The method

Works inside-out. Help someone see themselves clearly — their real ambition, their real friction — and strategy, team, and culture become obvious.

Primary audience

Founders and future founders (18-60+). The person performing someone else's version of success and stuck because of it.

Connection to LE

LE's line: "Work is the curriculum." Adi's: "The real work is you." At LE, work is how you learn. With Adi, YOU are the work.

Hard Rules

Non-negotiable. Applies to every agent, every draft, every platform.

  • TypographyNo em dashes (—) or en dashes (–) ever. In any content for posting. Replace with commas, periods, or restructure. All platforms, all formats — including internal docs.
  • Let's EnterpriseThe 95/5 rule. 95% thought leadership. 5% LE mentions — only obliquely (a student, an anecdote). No admissions CTAs. Never "apply now."
  • DraftingAsk 2-3 sharp questions before writing anything. One must request a specific story or incident. No anchor = no draft.
  • Feed disciplineNo promotional posts on personal feed. LE event invites, admissions pushes — never on personal LinkedIn or Instagram.
  • SeriesNo series fatigue. Never run 3+ posts on the same external person. Decay confirmed by analytics by post 3.

Color Do and Don't

Do

  • Start from cream, set type in navy, accent with gold.
  • Use Heritage Gold for print and letters, Bright Gold for social and screens.
  • Let gold be the loudest color — use sparingly so it stays special.
  • Put navy text on gold fills, cream text on navy and teal.
  • Keep teal in a supporting role, cooler and quieter than gold.

Don't

  • Introduce coral, red, orange, or any hue not on this page. (Confirmed ruled OUT.)
  • Set gold text on cream — 2.05:1, it fails accessibility.
  • Use white text on gold — 2.21:1, it also fails.
  • Let teal compete with gold or carry the headline.
  • Use Bright Gold on large fields — small accent areas only.

Type scale (web / System A)

Keep no more than two families on any single layout.

DisplayDM Serif · 44-56px
Heading 1DM Serif · 32-40px
Heading 2DM Serif · 24-28px
Heading 3 / labelInter 600 · 18-20px
Body copy. Line-height 1.6-1.7.Inter 400 · 16px
Caption / metaInter 500 · 12-13px

Type do

  • Two families per layout maximum. Serif for feeling, sans for clarity.
  • Sentence case for almost everything — in line with the voice.
  • Body at 16px web with 1.6-1.7 line-height. Never below 14px.
  • Reserve Caveat and Lora italic for rare, deliberate accents.

Type don't

  • Mix System A and System B fonts in the same asset.
  • Set long passages in ALL CAPS. Caps for short punch labels only.
  • Stack three or more typefaces on one piece.
  • Use DM Serif or Lato Black for tiny body text.
